About

Data first, then the story

My background runs from field geology to climate policy. I started with a BSc in Geology, worked with oil and gas well data, then moved into sustainability: building Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions baselines at a university sustainability office and running my own climate consultancy in Pakistan.

What I do best is collect messy data from many sources, check it, document it so it can be audited, and turn it into reports, KPIs and maps. I also enjoy teaching it, and have trained more than 100 people in carbon accounting, climate finance and sustainability practice.

I am now studying Tropical Hydrogeology and Environmental Engineering at TU Darmstadt, which adds groundwater modelling and climate adaptation to my sustainability toolkit. I am open to roles and projects in ESG and sustainability data, carbon and life cycle assessment, and geospatial climate analysis, and open to relocation.

Projects

  • NUST Campus GHG Inventory and Mitigation Planning

    Consolidated energy, mobility and waste activity data from multiple sources into a Scope 1, 2 and 3 baseline for a university campus. Found the biggest emission hotspots and supported the mitigation plan.

    GHG Protocol · Scope 1–3 · Excel · IPCC methods
  • Ongoing research

    Comparative LCA: Biochar vs. Crop-Residue Burning

    An OpenLCA model using Ecoinvent that compares climate, air-quality and resource impacts of two ways to handle crop residue. Covers scenario definition, life cycle inventory, impact assessment and hotspot interpretation.

    OpenLCA · Ecoinvent · LCI / LCIA · ISO 14040/44

  • Islamabad International Airport Carbon Footprint

    Assessed the airport's direct and indirect emission sources and supported scenario-based analysis of reduction options for transport infrastructure.

    Carbon footprint · Scenario analysis

  • Climate Vulnerability and Spatial Impact Analysis

    Combined environmental and geospatial datasets to map climate exposure and sensitivity and to identify where adaptation is needed most.

    ArcGIS Pro · QGIS · Climate risk

  • Groundwater Modelling Portfolio (MODFLOW 6)

    Three models built in Python with FloPy: a transient pumping test checked against the Theis solution, river and aquifer interaction under seasonal recharge, and Henry saltwater intrusion in a coastal aquifer. Each produces heads, budgets and concentration maps.

    View on GitHub
    MODFLOW 6 · FloPy · Python · Solute transport

  • Terrain Change Analysis with ArcGIS Pro

    Georeferenced geological maps, imported structural field measurements, and compared a high-resolution DGM5 elevation model with one interpolated from contours. Cut/fill analysis showed zones of erosion and deposition.

    View on GitHub
    ArcGIS Pro · DEM · Cut/fill · File geodatabase

  • GIS Site Suitability Analysis, Darmstadt

    Multi-criteria spatial assessment weighing geology, water bodies, distance to settlements, forest cover and slope.

    Multi-criteria analysis · Raster / vector

  • More ArcGIS work

    Three further GIS projects published on ArcGIS.

    ArcGIS project 1 · ArcGIS project 2 · ArcGIS project 3
    ArcGIS Online · Spatial analysis
